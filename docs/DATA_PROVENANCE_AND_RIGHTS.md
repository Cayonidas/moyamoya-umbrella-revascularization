# Data provenance and rights

The analytical inputs are structured extraction and adjudication files created for an umbrella review of published systematic reviews and meta-analyses of moyamoya disease/syndrome.

- No full-text journal articles are included.
- The frozen master workbook contains factual extraction fields, bibliographic metadata, methodological judgments, source locators, and limited source-verification excerpts.
- The independent full-text extraction workbook is required by the overlap module because it contains the frozen primary-study membership matrix used to reconstruct publication overlap.
- Third-party article titles, citation metadata, and source-verification excerpts remain subject to the rights of the original authors and publishers and are not relicensed by this repository.
- `data/moyamoya_umbrella_master_extraction_FROZEN_v2.xlsx` is the official frozen master analytical input.
- `data/moyamoya_umbrella_SAP_v1_analysis_specification.xlsx` is the prespecified analysis specification.
- The analysis does not require redistribution of the underlying journal PDFs.
